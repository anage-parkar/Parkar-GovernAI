Everything you need to govern, monitor, and audit your AI systems — from risk assessment to regulatory compliance.

EU AI Act ISO 42001 ISO 27001 NIST AI RMF GDPR HIPAA
🚀 Get started in 5 steps
1
Register your first AI use case Navigate to Governance → Use Cases. Create a new use case, classify its EU AI Act risk level, and assign framework compliance requirements.
2
Add your AI models to the inventory Go to Model Inventory, register every model in use, assign an approver, and document security assessments.
3
Import risks from curated databases In Risk Management, import from the IBM AI Risk Database (113 risks) or MIT AI Risk Repository — no blank sheet needed.
4
Map controls across frameworks Open Frameworks, assign owners to ISO 42001 / NIST AI RMF controls, and attach evidence to each satisfied control.
5
Generate your first compliance report Go to Reporting, choose your framework, upload your logo, and generate a board-ready PDF in under 30 seconds.
